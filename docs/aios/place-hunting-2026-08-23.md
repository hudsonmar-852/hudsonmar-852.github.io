# AIOS Place Hunting — 2026-08-23

## Objective
Find a comfortable, low-friction temporary home in Hong Kong for about 3 months, avoiding a rushed long-term lease while preserving flexibility for work and longer-term housing decisions.

## Current Target
- Stay length: target 3 months
- Budget target: HK$12,000–15,000 per 30 nights/month
- Preferred format: monthly hotel / serviced apartment / furnished studio
- Location priority: flexible across Hong Kong; Hong Kong Island preferred when value is comparable
- Convenience: near MTR, shops and daily necessities
- Space/comfort: more important than exact district
- Services preferred: furnished, Wi-Fi, housekeeping; all-inclusive utilities preferred
- Avoid: long fixed lease, complicated utility setup, unnecessary deposits/commitments
- Payment: card payment is useful, but any third-party card support should only be used with the cardholder’s explicit agreement

## AIOS Scoring Model
Score each option /100:
- Total monthly cost incl. mandatory fees/utilities: 25
- Room size & comfort: 20
- Included services (Wi-Fi, housekeeping, utilities): 15
- Lease flexibility / 1–3 month suitability: 15
- MTR & daily convenience: 10
- Room quality / photos / condition: 10
- Booking friction, deposit & cancellation terms: 5

## Required Data Before Booking
For every shortlisted room, AIOS should capture:
- Exact room name/type
- Net usable area in sq ft / m²
- Exact 90-night / 3-month total
- Service charge / taxes / admin fees
- Electricity / water / Wi-Fi inclusions and fair-use caps
- Housekeeping frequency + linen/towel policy
- Deposit / prepayment
- Cancellation / early termination
- Card acceptance and whether third-party card payment is allowed
- Laundry access
- Cooking facilities / microwave / fridge
- MTR walking time
- Latest room photos / virtual tour
- Earliest move-in date

## Decision Rule
Do not commit to a long-term lease during the current transition. Prefer a 1–3 month furnished stay with transparent all-in cost. Re-rank based on total 90-day cost rather than headline monthly rent.
